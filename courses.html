UGClass B Course one

Fresh learner — light vehicle

The full course for someone who has never driven. You start in a quiet place with dual controls beside you and finish holding a permit and the confidence to use it.

  • Controls and clutch. Moving off, stopping smoothly, gear changes, steering without drift.
  • Town driving. Junctions, roundabouts, lane discipline and reading Kampala traffic.
  • Hills and rough roads. Hill starts, murram surfaces, potholes and speed humps.
  • Parking. Parallel, reverse bay and hill parking, drilled until automatic.
  • Theory and the test. Free classes throughout, then preparation for exactly what the examiner asks.

UGAdvanced Course two

Advanced & defensive driving

For people who already hold a permit but know they are not really comfortable. This is the course that turns a licence holder into a driver.

  • Hazard anticipation. Spot the boda that has not indicated and the pedestrian about to step out, seconds before they move.
  • Space and escape. Holding a gap in dense traffic and always having somewhere to go.
  • Night and wet weather. Glare, unlit road users, standing water and reduced grip.
  • Emergency handling. Braking hard without losing the car, skid recovery and what to do after an incident.
  • Basic mechanics. Checks before a long trip, changing a tyre, and reading what the dashboard is telling you.

UGClass A Course three

Motorbike training

For boda riders who want to work legally and for private riders who want to survive the commute. Taught on marked training bikes, starting somewhere quiet.

  • Low-speed control. Balance, slow manoeuvres, clutch feel and U-turns in a tight space.
  • Braking. Front and rear together, in the dry and on loose murram.
  • Being seen. Lane position, mirrors, indicating early and staying out of blind spots.
  • Carrying a passenger. How the bike changes when someone is behind you, and how to brief them.
  • Rider permit. Processed by us, so you are working within the law.

Skills modules

Take one on its own, or fold it into a course.

Already driving but weak on one thing? Book just that.

Parking lessons

Parallel parking, reverse bay, hill parking and tight-compound reversing. Reference points you can actually remember under pressure.

Night driving

Headlight discipline, judging distance in the dark, coping with oncoming glare and spotting unlit pedestrians and cyclists. Taught after sunset.

Defensive driving

The mindset that keeps good drivers out of other people’s accidents. Often taken by companies for staff who drive on duty.

Basic mechanics

Oil, coolant, tyre pressure and tread, battery terminals, changing a wheel, and what each warning light means before it strands you.

Theory classes — free

Road signs, markings, right of way, speed limits and test preparation. Free with every course, and you may sit in as many times as you need.

Refresher lessons

For drivers who have been off the road for years, or who bought a car and have not touched one since the test. A few hours usually does it.

Course questions

The things people ask us first.

Ask on WhatsApp Register
How much does a course cost?

It depends on the course and how many lessons you need, so we quote per learner rather than publishing one figure that fits nobody. You will have the number in writing before you pay anything, and the first lesson is free either way.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Most learners pay across the course rather than all at once. Agree the arrangement with your branch when you register.

How many lessons will I need?

Honestly, it varies. Some people are comfortable in a couple of weeks and some need longer. After your free first lesson the instructor will give you a realistic estimate rather than a sales figure.

Do you teach automatic as well as manual?

Tell us which you intend to drive and we will arrange the right car. Learning on a manual leaves you able to drive both, so we usually recommend it.

Can my company book defensive driving for staff?

Yes. Group defensive-driving training for staff who drive on duty is something we run regularly. Call us to arrange dates and numbers.

Are theory classes really free?

Yes, and there is no cap on attendance. Come to as many as you need until the signs and rules stick.
