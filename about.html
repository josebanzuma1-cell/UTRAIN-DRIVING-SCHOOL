About the school

A driving school built around the driver you become.

Five branches across Kampala. Practical training, free theory, and the permit handled from start to finish.

Our story

Learn well. Go well.

U-Train Driving School exists because too many people in Kampala are handed a permit before they are handed any judgement. They pass a test, they get the paper, and then they meet their first wet roundabout at night with a boda on the inside.

We teach the other way round. Control first, confidence second, paperwork last. Our instructors sit beside learners on the same roads they will drive every day — Jinja Road at rush hour, the Kireka slope, the Kiwatule stage at dusk — because that is where the lesson actually lands.

What began as a single training car is now five locations, a full set of courses from motorbike to defensive driving, and a permit desk that takes the queueing off your hands. The promise has not changed: we don’t just teach you to drive, we prepare you for the road of life.

What drives us

Three things we will not compromise on.

Our mission

To turn nervous beginners into drivers who are genuinely safe — through patient practical training, free theory, and honest assessment of when someone is ready.

Our vision

A Kampala where a U-Train sticker on a car means the person behind the wheel was taught properly — and where good driving is the normal thing, not the exception.

Our values

Patience with every learner. Honesty about fees and timelines. Respect for the road and everyone on it. And never signing off a driver who is not ready.

Inside the lesson

What an hour with a U-Train instructor actually looks like.

No sitting in a yard revving an engine. Every lesson has a plan, and you leave knowing what the next one covers.

The first ten minutes

A quick recap of last time, then the instructor names the one thing this lesson is for — hill starts, mirror discipline, a roundabout you struggled with.

The drive

You drive. The instructor talks only when it helps, and the dual controls are there so a mistake is a lesson rather than a crash.

The hard bit, repeated

Whatever you fumbled gets done again, and again, until your hands do it without your head. That is the whole trick.

The last five minutes

An honest word on where you are, and what to practise or read before the next session. Free theory classes fill the gaps.

Our training cars

Dual controls, L plates, roof signs.

Marked, insured and maintained — so other drivers give you room while you learn.
