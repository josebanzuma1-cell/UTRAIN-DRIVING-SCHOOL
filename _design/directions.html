Four ways this site could look.

Each panel below is real, working markup at full size — not a picture of an idea. The nav, headline, buttons and one content section are rendered in that direction's own type, colour and layout, using U-Train's actual words. Scroll through, then tell me which one to build out.

The honest diagnosis first. What I shipped is competent but it is decorated rather than designed: five motifs fighting each other, every section a card grid, heavy type everywhere, and no photography. The directions below each commit to one idea and carry it the whole way. Two of them need real photographs to work; two do not.
01

Night Shift

Cinematic and quiet. Full-bleed photography of Kampala roads at dusk, hairline-thin type, almost no colour. Borrowed from Porsche Driving, which carries an entire site on one aerial photograph and a light-weight headline.

What actually makes the difference

Direction is half of it. The other half is photography, and it is the single biggest reason the current site reads as a template. SVG cars and gradients can only ever look like SVG cars and gradients.

  • Six photographs would carry the whole site: a learner's hands on the wheel, an instructor talking through a junction, a marked car on a real Kampala road, a theory class, one branch front, one portrait of a learner holding a permit.
  • Shot on a decent phone in good light, early morning or late afternoon, they beat any stock library — because they are visibly Kampala and visibly you.
  • Directions 02 and 04 do not need them and can ship as they are. 01 and 03 depend on them and should not be built until the photographs exist.
