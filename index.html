What we offer

Everything from first gear to full permit.

One school handles the driving, the theory and the paperwork — so you are not sent from office to office.

01

Practical road training

Real roads, real traffic. You start on quiet stretches and work up to Kampala’s busiest junctions with an instructor beside you the whole way.

02

Parking lessons

Parallel, reverse bay and hill parking drilled until they stop being frightening. The skill most new drivers are judged on, taught properly.

03

Night driving

Headlight discipline, judging distance in the dark, reading unlit road users and handling glare. Taught after sunset, where it counts.

04

Free theory classes

Road signs, rules and test preparation — included with every course at no extra charge, and open to you until you are confident.

05

Basic mechanics

Check the oil, change a tyre, read the dashboard and know when a noise matters. Enough to keep you safe and stop you being overcharged.

06

Defensive driving

Anticipate the boda that has not indicated, hold your space in traffic and plan an escape. The habits that keep good drivers out of crashes.

How it works

Four stops between here and your permit.

No mystery, no being sent away and told to come back tomorrow.

  1. 1

    Register

    Walk into any branch or message us. Bring an ID and a passport photo. Your first practical lesson that day is free.

  2. 2

    Learn

    Theory classes run free alongside your practical lessons. You drive at the times that suit your week.

  3. 3

    Test

    We only put you forward when you are genuinely ready, and we prepare you for exactly what the examiner looks for.

  4. 4

    Drive

    We process the permit and hand it to you. Come back any time for a refresher or a class extension.

Questions

Before you register.

If your question is not here, call us. We would rather answer it now than have you guess.

Ask on WhatsApp
Is the first lesson really free?

Yes. Register at any branch and your first practical lesson costs nothing. It is there so you can meet the instructor and sit in the car before you commit to the course.

Do I need to know anything before I start?

No. Most of our learners have never held a steering wheel. You start in a quiet place with an instructor who has dual controls, and nothing is rushed.

How long does a course take?

It depends on how often you come and how quickly you settle. Learners who drive two or three times a week usually finish a fresh-learner course in a few weeks. We will give you an honest estimate after your first lesson.

Can you process my permit if I learned elsewhere?

Yes. Permit processing, renewal and class extensions are open to everyone, whether or not you trained with U-Train.

What times can I come?

We are open Monday to Sunday and we schedule around your work. Early morning, midday, after work and weekend slots are all available — including night-driving lessons after dark.

Do you teach motorbikes?

Yes. Motorbike training runs alongside the car courses and includes the rider permit, which matters for boda riders working legally.
