What to bring

  • A national ID or passport
  • Two passport photographs
  • Your current permit, if you hold one
  • Comfortable shoes you can feel the pedals in

What happens after you send

  1. 1. We reply to confirm the branch and a start time.
  2. 2. You take the free lesson — nothing paid yet.
  3. 3. We agree the fee and a schedule that fits your week.
  4. 4. Free theory classes start straight away.
No pressure

Judge us before you pay us.

The free first lesson is not a trick. It is there because the only honest way to choose a driving school is to sit in the car with the instructor.

No deposit to register

Registering costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. The fee is agreed after the free lesson, not before.

One quoted figure

You get the whole number up front — lessons and, where it applies, what the permit will cost on top. Nothing is added later.

Change instructor freely

If you do not click with your instructor, say so and we will move you. Learning to drive is hard enough without that.
