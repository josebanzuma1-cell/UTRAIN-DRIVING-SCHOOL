The process

Four steps, and we do three of them.

  1. 1

    Bring your documents

    Come to any branch with your ID and photographs, or send clear photos of them on WhatsApp and we will tell you what else is needed.

  2. 2

    We file the application

    Forms completed, fees paid to the authority, and your file lodged. Errors here are what cause the long delays, so we check twice.

  3. 3

    Test & biometrics

    Where a test or a data capture is required, we book it and prepare you for it. You only turn up on the day.

  4. 4

    Collect your permit

    We follow it up and call you when it is ready. Pick it up at your branch.

What to bring

Have these ready and we can start the same day.

The list is short, but a missing item is usually what sends people home. Check before you travel.

For a first permit

  • National ID or passport
  • Two passport photographs
  • Certificate of completion from a licensed driving school
  • A phone number for the SMS alerts

For renewal or an extension

  • Your current or expired permit
  • National ID or passport
  • A passport photograph
  • For an extension, training on the new class where it is required

Please note. Requirements, government fees and processing times are set by the national licensing authority and do change. Call us on 0755 938383 before you travel and we will confirm what applies today — and what our own service fee is, separately from the authority’s charges.

Permit classes

Which class do you actually need?

The class on your permit decides what you are allowed to drive. Tell us the vehicle and we will confirm the class before anything is filed.

Common Ugandan driving permit classes and who holds them
ClassCoversUsually held byWe can
Class A Motorcycles Boda riders and private motorcyclists Train & process
Class B Light motor vehicles — cars, pickups, dual-purpose Private drivers, company staff, ride-hailing drivers Train & process
Goods vehicles Medium and heavy goods vehicles Lorry and truck drivers Extend & process
Omnibuses Light, medium and heavy passenger vehicles Taxi, coaster and bus drivers Extend & process
Plant & tractors Tractors and construction or engineering plant Farm and site operators Extend & process

Exact class codes, weight limits and passenger limits are defined by the licensing authority, not by us. This table is a guide to point you at the right one — we confirm the precise class for your vehicle when you come in.

Permit questions

Straight answers.

If something here does not match what you have been told elsewhere, call us and we will check it properly rather than guess.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does a permit take?

That depends on the authority’s processing times, which move around. We will give you a realistic window when you hand in your documents, and we call you the moment it is ready rather than leaving you to check.

My permit expired years ago. Is it too late?

No. Long-expired permits are routine for us. Bring the old permit and your ID and we will tell you exactly what the renewal involves in your case.

Can I get a permit without attending a driving school?

A certificate of completion from a licensed driving school is part of what is required for a first permit. That is one of the reasons the school and the permit desk sit under one roof here.

Do I have to retake a test to extend my class?

It depends on the class you are moving to. Where training or a test is required we arrange both; where it is not, the extension is paperwork. We will tell you which applies before you commit.

What do your services cost?

There are two separate things: the government fees set by the authority, and our service fee for handling it. We quote both clearly and we do not add anything afterwards.

Can you process a permit for someone upcountry?

Often, yes, depending on what needs to be done in person. Message us with the details and we will tell you honestly whether it is workable.
